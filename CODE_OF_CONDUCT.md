# Community Conduct

Clef discussions affect creators, administrators, requesters, and implementers with conflicting interests. Critique mechanisms, evidence, claims, and consequences directly. Do not harass participants, expose private catalog or contract material, publish credentials, or convert disagreement into allegations about a person.

Maintainers may remove material that compromises safety, privacy, or useful technical review. Report sensitive conduct or security concerns through the repository's private security-advisory channel.
