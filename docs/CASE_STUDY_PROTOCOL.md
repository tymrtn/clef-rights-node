# Public case-study protocol

The first case must test an organizational deployment, not stage a promotional demo.

## Eligibility

- A named label or publisher can evidence authority for one identified work or recording.
- A named creator and a separate requester consent to the bounded use and proposed publication.
- The request has a real purpose, territory, term, policy, human decision, and feasible external payment path.
- Every required composition, master, lyric, performance, voice, and likeness right is either evidenced or declared out of scope.

## Required record chain

Deployment configuration; catalog identifier and client assignment; private evidence reference and digest; versioned policy; verified authority; requester declaration; named review; signed obligations; lifecycle events; publication evidence; compliance review; external payment evidence; beneficiary payouts; reconciliation; independent verification output.

## Consent and disclosure gates

Every named participant approves its name, role, quoted language, financial detail, receipt fields, linked evidence, and redactions before publication. Private contracts, credentials, personal addresses, unpublished communications, and unrelated catalog data stay outside the public repository.

Contradictory, missing, late, rejected, and inconclusive evidence remains visible in the agreed record. A participant may withdraw from publication before release without erasing the node's private operational history.

## Publication standard

Publish the source revision, methods, deployment time, policy scope, decision trail, approved redacted receipts, publication evidence, financial reconciliation, exceptions, reviewer findings, and unresolved risks. Do not imply that one case establishes legal validity, universal interoperability, or institutional endorsement.
