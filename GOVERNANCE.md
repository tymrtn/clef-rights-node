# Governance

Clef Rights Node is public-interest infrastructure. Its purpose is to help creators and rightsholders express permissions, prove decisions, and account for payments made through external systems.

## Commitments

- No royalty percentage is required by the protocol.
- No catalog content is used for model training by the reference node.
- Organizations retain control of private catalog, contract, identity, and payout data.
- Policies, receipts, and exports remain portable.
- Protocol changes preserve explicit truth states and independent verification.
- Governance should maintain meaningful representation for working artists and songwriters.

Technical maintainers may reject changes that weaken tenant isolation, disguise uncertainty, create platform lock-in, or convert evidence into unsupported legal claims.
